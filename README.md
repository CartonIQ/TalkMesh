## ✨ Features

- 🌐 **True Mesh Network** — Messages hop from phone to phone (up to 4 hops). If A and C are out of range, B relays the message automatically.
- 🔒 **End-to-End Encryption** — Direct messages use ECDH (P-256) and AES-256-GCM. Private rooms use PBKDF2 key derivation.
- 📻 **Radar** — See nearby peers, their vibe, and how many hops away they are.
- 🆘 **SOS Broadcast** — Hold the SOS button to send your GPS location, siren, and flashlight to everyone in range.
- 🎮 **Local Fun** — Play Tic-Tac-Toe, share stories (vanish after 24h), or post anonymously on the local board.
- 🎙️ **Walkie-Talkie** — Record and send 15-second voice notes to direct neighbours.
- 📁 **Mesh Drop** — Send files directly to a nearby peer without internet.
- 🔋 **Battery Saver** — Custom search/pause cycles to save battery while keeping the mesh alive.
